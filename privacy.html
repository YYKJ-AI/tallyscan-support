Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026

Your receipts are stored on your device. Online AI is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings.

Online AI sends receipt information outside your device. Turning it off stops new AI recognition requests; information already sent cannot be recalled.

1. Information the app processes

TallyScan processes receipt images and detected text, merchant names, transaction dates, totals, currencies, categories, business purposes, attendees you enter, original-image locations and editing timestamps. These records and original images are kept in the app’s local storage. Merchant preferences and aggregate usage counts are stored on your device.

2. On-device recognition and online AI

On-device recognition uses Apple’s Vision frameworks. When online AI is enabled, normalized receipt images and selected detected text are sent to the TallyScan gateway at resetbell.dev. The current recognition configuration sends detected text to aiapiv2.pekpik.com for AI processing. Gateway/provider configuration may evolve; material processing changes will be reflected in this policy and the app.

You can turn off online AI in Settings before importing a receipt and continue with on-device recognition, manual editing and data export. If online recognition is unavailable, saved on-device results are kept. AI output may be inaccurate and must be checked against the original.

3. Installation security and Apple services

The gateway verifies Apple installation/transaction proofs and request signatures to restrict abuse. Installation public keys, replay-prevention nonces and quota metadata may be processed for that purpose. This metadata is separate from the receipt content. Apple handles App Store downloads, any subscription transactions and subscription status. TallyScan does not receive your complete payment-card details from Apple. Apple device backups and files you choose to save in iCloud are subject to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

4. Storage, retention and deletion

You can delete individual receipts in the app and export a copy of your records. Uninstalling the app removes its local container according to iOS behavior; copies in exported archives, other apps or device backups remain under your control. Restoring a TallyScan backup does not automatically submit its receipts to AI.

For this preview, a fixed retention/deletion schedule for gateway security metadata, infrastructure logs and the external AI provider has not been confirmed. We do not promise zero retention or that externally processed data is never used for training. If these arrangements do not meet your needs, disable online AI and avoid sending sensitive documents. Contact us about externally processed data; we will investigate what can be located and deleted, subject to the services involved and applicable obligations.

5. Analytics and sharing

The app keeps aggregate action counts on your device. They do not include receipt contents or per-user/device identifiers. Usage counts are not automatically sent to us; you may choose to share them for support. TallyScan does not include advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. Exporting or sharing receipts sends the files only to the destination you choose. Exported archives contain private financial information: store and share them carefully.

6. Website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or tracking cookies; GitHub may process standard hosting connection logs under its own privacy policy. If you email support, we receive the information and attachments you choose to send. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or unnecessary personal data. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone.

7. Your choices and questions

No TallyScan account is required. You can disable online AI, edit or delete local records, export your data, and contact support about privacy. The app is intended for expense record keeping and is not directed to children. TallyScan does not provide tax or legal advice.
