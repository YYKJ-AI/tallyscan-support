Support / 使用支持

Questions about scanning, correcting, exporting or restoring your receipts?

Email support / 联系支持

Public issue tracker / 公开问题反馈

Please include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you did and what you expected. Do not post private receipts, card details or personal information in public issues.

How recognition works / 怎样识别

Add one receipt per image, check the merchant, date, total and currency, then add the category and business purpose. New receipts are saved locally before automatic AI checking. Only changed or uncertain fields need confirmation. Tap Done when you have checked the result.

Online AI / 在线 AI

Online AI is enabled by default. Turn it off in Settings to use on-device recognition. Cancelling or disabling AI keeps the local result and cannot recall data already sent. AI completion is not a guarantee of accuracy. Read the privacy policy.

Exports and backups / 导出与备份

A Tax Pack contains a receipt spreadsheet, category totals, PDF evidence and original images. Full-data backup export is available without a subscription. Backups made by current app versions include a machine-readable manifest for restoration; older CSV/image-only archives may not support complete restoration. Keep a backup before changing devices.

Missing records / 记录打不开

Do not uninstall the app if existing records cannot be opened. Contact support with the error and your app version. Uninstalling may remove local records.

Subscriptions / 订阅

Apple manages subscription billing and restoration. Availability, displayed prices and trial eligibility depend on the installed app version and Apple account. Preview sandbox transactions are distinct from production purchases. For an Apple purchase or refund, use your Apple subscription settings or Apple’s support process.

Scope / 使用边界

TallyScan keeps records; it is not tax advice. Categories and calendar-year reports do not determine deductibility. Purpose timestamps come from your device clock, not an independent certification service.
